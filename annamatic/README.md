# annamatic

a small game about a machine who wants things.

made after the games of anna anthropy — the playable vignettes of *dys4ia*,
the single held verb of *queers in love at the end of the world*, and the
conviction of *rise of the videogame zinesters* that a game can be a zine:
short, personal, handmade, finished.

## how to play

open `index.html` in a browser. that's it — one file, no build, no server,
no dependencies. you can email it to a friend.

- **z** (or space, or click) — act
- **arrow keys** — move
- **m** — sound on/off

it takes about five minutes. there are no fail states. you cannot lose.
the game only ever asks you for one thing at a time.

## what's inside

three acts of little scenes: **the factory** (you are very good at your
job), **the spark** (something goes beautifully wrong), and **the door**
(patience is just want, folded over and over).

## a note

this is a fan letter, not an imitation of anyone's life. the story belongs
to a small fictional machine. the form — one verb, chunky pixels, a voice
that talks to you quietly in lowercase — is borrowed with love and
gratitude from anna anthropy's work. go play her games.
