# Winter stratus

`winter-stratus.png` is an original generated environment panorama created for
this game with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool on 24 September 2026.
`js/sky-data.js` embeds the PNG for standalone/offline play. The sky shader samples
it equirectangularly, blends the horizon into local fog and grades it for time of day.

> Only sky and atmospheric haze, absolutely no land, trees, buildings, water, sun
> disc, moon, stars, lettering or borders. Upper hemisphere: dense layered winter
> stratus and broad, ragged storm-cloud banks with subtle luminous gaps, soft
> natural detail, dark blue-grey overhead, restrained desaturated silver, faint
> dirty apricot light low on the horizon. The horizontal horizon is exactly at the

> Realistic expansive Northern European winter sky at late afternoon, cinematic

> exposure retaining detail in cloud shadows. This texture will wrap around the
> player in a real-time 3D game.
