# Authored surface textures

These original PNGs were generated for this game using the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool on 24 September 2026. They are retained unchanged and embedded in `js/material-data.js` for offline play. The renderer uses mipmaps and restrained blending for the ice, and world-space vertical mapping for the siding. These are albedo textures, not photographs of real locations.

## lake-ice.png

Prompt:

> Use case: game-material. Generate one production-ready seamless square albedo texture of a frozen Scandinavian lake under thin wind-scoured snow. Orthographic straight down, physically flat surface, no horizon or perspective. Pale desaturated blue-grey freshwater ice, irregular cloudy trapped-air patches, very fine scattered white hairline fractures, small frosted islands and granular snow dust, subtly transparent dark mineral-like areas. Calm solid opaque-enough frozen winter ice, NOT liquid water, no ripples, no waves, no round boulders, no dramatic lightning cracks. Restrained moderate contrast with no obvious repeating motif. No baked directional light, no cast shadows, no highlights, no vignette. Even diffuse albedo, seamlessly tileable on all four sides. Entire frame filled edge to edge with this surface. 1024 x 1024. No text, labels, borders or objects.

## falu-siding.png

Prompt:

> Use case: game-material. A seamless square production albedo texture of aged Scandinavian Falu-red vertical wooden siding, straight orthographic front view, no perspective. Eight to ten narrow vertical sawn pine planks across the frame, faded deep iron-oxide red paint, subtle fine wood grain, grey-brown exposed grain at worn edges, small dark nailheads, thin straight black seams. Quiet understated weathering and slight individual board variation, no dramatic peeling and no giant scratches. Authentic rural Swedish farmhouse material in cold winter, but NO snow, no ice, no scenery. Fairly dark muted brick red and earthy burgundy, not orange. Flat even diffuse neutral lighting; no baked light gradients, no shadows, no ambient occlusion vignette. Seamlessly tileable on all four edges, each plank continues vertically through top and bottom. Full frame, no text, labels, objects, frame or border. 1024 by 1024.
