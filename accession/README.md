# ACCESSION (working title)

A turn-based roguelike about getting out of a museum too large to have a floor
plan. Design phase: nothing is playable yet.

- `research/` holds the seven research briefs the design is built on.
- `prototypes/` holds throwaway scripts that back numbers in the design.
- `DESIGN.md` (in progress) will hold the design sketch and the decisions to go
  through before building.
