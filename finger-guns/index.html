Finger Guns

Seven playable sketches of a casual, fast first-person shooter, each controlled a different way.

A shooter asks you to do four jobs at once: move, look, aim and fire. With a mouse and keyboard, or two thumbsticks, that means two hands doing different things at speed, and that is most of what keeps people out of the genre. Each sketch gives some of the jobs to the game or folds them into one gesture. Rounds last a minute, you have three hearts, and every sketch plays on a touch screen, all but the last with one finger.

M mutes and Esc pauses. Best scores are kept in this browser.

Everything is drawn in code: a small grid raycaster for the walls, flat billboards for everything else, and WebAudio for the sound.
