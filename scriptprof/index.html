SCRIPTPROF

a lab for generating puzzle games

Write the rules as rewrite patterns.
Let a search agent sit the exam.
Keep the mutants that still mean something.

PuzzleScript is a language of local rewrites over a grid. A player walking into a crate is one pattern; the crate inheriting that force is the replacement. Whole games fit in a page of text, and a breadth-first student can mark them.

ScriptProf is a small instrument built from three papers: ScriptDoctor's compile–playtest loop, GAVEL's evolutionary wrapping of a language model, and PuzzleJAX's observation that those rewrites are a benchmark, not just a toy.
