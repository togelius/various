The panel on the right is what it is thinking — the move it chose, how many frames of it are still committed, and whether it is actually getting anywhere.

How it moves

Navigation is solved, not learned. A backward Dijkstra pass over the level gives every standable tile a cost to go, including the wall-cling states, and the agent simply tries to spend it.

Why the trace matters

A short-horizon planner dithers: when every move is bad, the least bad one is to not move. Watch the line go flat on the lava and in the spike corridor — that is the failure mode. The red bar is how long it has been stuck, and it eventually forces the agent to accept a hit and go.

What it measured

Over 36 full runs it finished the game about a quarter of the time and beat all three bosses 57 times out of 60. The hard parts are not the bosses — they are the spike corridor, the lava climb, and stage one's crumbling blocks.
