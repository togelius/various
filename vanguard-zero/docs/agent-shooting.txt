
============================================================
  Is there a reason to shoot anything?
============================================================

The agent plays the game twice: once as it normally does, once with `noShoot`
set, which suppresses the trigger in both the level and the boss arena and
changes nothing else. If the two configurations come out the same, combat is
decoration.

  node tools/agent/report.js --stage=N --trials=8 --lives=9 --seconds=250
  node tools/agent/report.js --stage=N --trials=8 --lives=9 --seconds=250 \
       --params='{"noShoot":1}'


BEFORE: enemies respawned the frame they died
------------------------------------------------------------
streamEnemies cleared the spawn's live handle on death but never marked the
spawn, so the next frame's spawn window rebuilt the same enemy. Killing bought
nothing that lasted, and the numbers said so - refusing to shoot was *cheaper*
through the level:

  stage 1, level sections only     shoots    can't shoot
    damage per run                  7.8 hp       5.3 hp
    time per run                    23.6 s       25.7 s

That is the whole complaint in one row. The only thing shooting was good for
was the boss, which cannot be run past.


AFTER: a kill lasts until you lose a life
------------------------------------------------------------
  stage 1, level sections only     shoots    can't shoot
    damage per run                  6.1 hp       5.5 hp
    time per run                    23.8 s       25.7 s

  stage 3, level sections only     shoots    can't shoot
    damage per run                 16.4 hp      22.9 hp
    deaths per run                    0.0          0.1
    "Turret + shielders" damage      18 hp        39 hp
    health returned by drops        5.5 hp       0.0 hp

Stage 1 is a wash and stage 3 is not, which is the right shape: the payoff
scales with how many enemies are actually in the way. Stage 1's opening was
deliberately thinned out during the difficulty rebalance, so there is little
there to kill - the agent finds 2.9 kills in the whole stage against 3.8 in
stage 3.

Two caveats worth keeping in mind when reading the stage 1 row:

- The agent fires on a blind duty cycle rather than aiming, so its kill count
  is a floor, not a ceiling. It cannot demonstrate an incentive it does not
  act on. A player who chooses to clear a room will see a larger effect than
  this measurement can.
- Changing the drop rolls changes how much of the random stream every other
  system consumes, so the two arms are effectively different seeds. Do not
  read differences under about 1 hp as real.


WHAT ACTUALLY HURTS YOU
------------------------------------------------------------
takeHit carries a source tag now, so the report can say what the damage came
from. Over four runs each, at full agent capability:

  stage 1     contact 45%   shot 29%   boss 26%
  stage 3     shot 54%      contact 43%   boss 3%

Spikes contribute nothing at all - the agent prices a spike bed at 900 and
simply never touches one, which a person will not manage. But every point of
damage the level deals comes from an enemy: 74% of stage 1's total and 97% of
stage 3's is something that could have been killed first. That is the ceiling
on what engaging can save, and it is nearly the whole bill.
