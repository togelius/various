ANANKE

a game of necessity

You cannot make anything happen.
You can only make things impossible.

The figures below are not characters. They have no plans. At every hour each of them may step to any open neighbour — or stand still. The game does not play out one future. It holds all of them at once, and draws each figure as the cloud of every place it could still turn out to be.

Your only power is prohibition. Forbid a cell from a given hour onward and every future that used it ceases to exist. Prune until the thing you want is not merely possible but necessary — true in every future that remains.

Prune too far and no future remains at all. A world with no possible history is a contradiction, and a contradiction is a loss.
