CLACK STACK: what your write-up should cover

Write the rules of the game down in your own words, as you found them by playing. For each topic below, say what you
saw and what you think the rule is (a guess is fine; say it is one). Topics with nothing to say: say so.

 1. Letting go: how a tile gets from the hand to where it ends up.
 2. Aiming: where the hand can hold a tile, and how it moves, for a square and for a triangle.
 3. Squares and point-up triangles: when they stay where they land.
 4. Points and corners.
 5. Point-down triangles: when they stay.
 6. Tiles that touch the tower and still fall off: when, and what the game shows.
 7. Tiles joined to each other: does it matter how they are joined?
 8. Triangles in the hand: which way up they come down, and what decides it.
 9. The tower itself falling over: when it happens, and what makes it more likely.
10. A run: the box of tiles, the ring round the tile in the hand, how a run ends, and the score.
