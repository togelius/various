# FERAL

A one-button action game about a feral ferret who has already decided the
clothesline is theirs. Socks, eggs, and other ferrets come in from both
sides, some of them along the ground and some of them along the line. Lunge
and you take the loot or bite the rival. A boot and, later, a dog walk
through as well. Lunging into either of those is a mistake. Letting them
pass is not.

A lunge a moment early still meets whatever is on its way. Five perfect
ones — timed as the target reaches you, when a small gold ring is showing —
and the ferret breaks into a war dance, hopping and arching and clearing the
yard. Swing at empty grass and you stumble. Stand still and a rival will bite.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It is one file with no dependencies. It loads
Fraunces and Nunito from Google Fonts if it can and uses fallback fonts if
not. Your best score is kept in the browser's local storage.

## Controls

Any one of these is enough on its own.

- **Tap or click a side.** The ferret lunges that way and chooses the bite.
  Aim up, on the line and the sky, to take the clothesline. Aim down, in the
  grass, to commit to the ground — which is how a boot or a dog gets bitten.
- **Space** or **Z** chooses the bite for you and will not go for a boot or
  a dog. **A** and **D**, or the arrow keys, lunge low. **Q** and **E** lunge
  up on the line. **W** and **S** pick a side on that height.
- A finger does the same thing as the mouse: tap a side, high or low.
- **Enter**, click, or tap to start and to play again. **Esc** or **P**
  pauses, **M** mutes. On a phone held upright the game asks you to turn it
  sideways; it still plays if you don't.

## How it's drawn

There are no image assets. The yard, the houses, the shed, the coop, the
line and every ferret are drawn on a canvas. The ferrets are long and low,
with a mask, a short tail-tip and the arched hop of a weasel war dance.
Sable, silver, cinnamon, panda and albino coats show up in the raids. Sound
is WebAudio: a clothespeg, a bite, a dook, a woof, and a quiet dusk.
